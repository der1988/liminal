# Third-party assets

`public/models/riot-unit.glb` is the `Soldier.glb` character and animation asset distributed with the Three.js examples, originally from Mixamo. It is used as an integrated character in this experience, with runtime material adjustments; no claim of original authorship is made.

Source (Three.js r180): https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
Example and credits: https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_animation_skinning_blending.html
